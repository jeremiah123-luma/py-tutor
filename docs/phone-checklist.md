# iPhone checklist (about 10 minutes)

Things a desktop browser can't prove. Do them once on the real phone.

1. Open the site in Safari, enter the passcode, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it from the home screen icon.
2. Pick **Talk**, start a lesson, tap the screen. You should hear the definition within about 2 seconds.
3. Allow the microphone when asked. When the ring turns green, ask "what's a key?" out loud. It should answer out loud.
4. Tap while it's talking. It should stop right away and start listening.
5. Leave it for 2 minutes without touching it. The screen should stay on.
6. In the car, on Bluetooth or CarPlay: do one full question-and-answer. Check that the tutor's voice comes out of the car speakers and that it doesn't interrupt itself.
7. On the laptop, finish one lesson in Type mode. Then open the phone app: the home screen should show that progress.

If any step fails, note the step number and what happened.
